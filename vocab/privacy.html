Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RIM Vocab (the "App"), provided by Rim N Hoff (the "Developer" and data controller), collects, uses, and protects information. By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Advertising ID: collected by Google AdMob to deliver and measure ads.
  • Device information: OS version, device model, etc., used for ad delivery and error analysis.
  • Learning data: word history, progress, and favorites are stored only on your device and are never transmitted externally.

The App requires no sign-up or login and does not directly collect personally identifying information such as your name or email.

2. Purpose & Legal Basis

  • Serving and measuring ads — based on your consent for personalized ads (EEA/UK/Switzerland), or our legitimate interest for non-personalized ads.
  • Providing and improving learning features.
  • Processing and validating in-app purchases.

3. Notifications

The App may use notification permission to send study reminders. Notifications run on your device, and you can revoke the permission anytime in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Please review their privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Consent for Users in the EEA, UK & Switzerland

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before serving personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time within the App: Settings → Manage ad consent (privacy options).

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because learning data is stored only on your device, you can erase it by uninstalling the App. For advertising data, use the controls below or contact us.

  • Limit ad personalization: device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Reset your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Withdraw ad consent: App Settings → Manage ad consent.
  • View purchase history in your Google Play account.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Learning data is stored only in your device storage and is removed when the App is uninstalled. Data collected by third-party services such as AdMob is retained and deleted according to their policies.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 16 (or the minimum age defined by your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when necessary. Changes are posted on this page and the "Last updated" date above is revised.
